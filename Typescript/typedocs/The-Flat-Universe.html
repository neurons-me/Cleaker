The Flat Universe ​

Reality has one primitive: distinction.

0 / 1 · A / not-A · occupy / not occupy

Everything computable is built from that. Hierarchy is not fundamental. It is emergent. Dimensions, geometry, complexity, consciousness — all are iterations of a binary decision.

Movement is change of occupation.

A → not-A

The base needs no purpose. No intention. No direction. Only one rule: allow distinction.

If you can compute it, you cannot lose it.Everything that is possible, is allowed.

distinction → information → geometry → landscape

This is why cleaker operates from a flat namespace tree rather than a strict hierarchy. The tree is a convenience for routing — not a statement about reality. Identity is not "above" or "below" context. Identity is the binding to context.

cleaker(me) — one operation. One primitive.
